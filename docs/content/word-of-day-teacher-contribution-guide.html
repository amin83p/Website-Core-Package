Each teacher can contribute words to the school Word of the Day program. This guide explains what to fill in, what you can skip, and how to save your work before you send it for review and approval.

Why contribute?

Approved words appear on the public Word of the Day page and in school dashboards. Your entries help students learn vocabulary in context. Before you create another word, check the contribution status at the top of the form so you know how many entries you have used this year.

What you need (required)

Before you submit, make sure the entry includes:

  • Word — the headword students will see.
  • Word type — select every type that applies (Noun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb, Prefix, Suffix, Phrase, or Other). If the word works as more than one part of speech, turn on each applicable type.
  • Pronunciation — phonetics or a simple pronunciation guide (for example, syllables or IPA if you use it).
  • Meaning — a clear definition students can understand.
  • At least one example — type a sentence in the examples box and click Add to list. You can add more than one.

What is not required (optional)

  • Photo, audio, and video URL — helpful but not required.
  • Difficulty — reviewers may set or adjust this later. If you are unsure, leave Medium selected (the default).

Step-by-step

  1. Go to School → Word of the Day (open the list) and click New word (or open New Word of the Day directly).
  2. Read your contribution status for the current year at the top of the page.
  3. Enter the word, select all word types that apply, and add pronunciation and meaning.
  4. Write each example sentence and click Add to list so at least one example appears in the list.
  5. Add photo, audio, or video only if you have them ready.
  6. Click Save draft if you want to finish later. Nothing is sent for review yet.
  7. When you are sure the entry is complete, click Save & submit so reviewers can approve it.

Save draft vs Save & submit

  • Save draft — keeps the word in saved status. Use this while you are still writing or checking your work.
  • Save & submit — sends the word into the review queue. Use this only when you are confident the required fields are correct.
  • If you are editing a word that is already submitted or approved, you will usually see Save only (not Save & submit). Save your corrections; workflow actions are handled by reviewers.

After you submit

Your word moves to submitted status. A reviewer will approve, reject, or return it for changes. If the same word already exists in your organization, the app will warn you and will not save a duplicate.
